Assembly overview — not to scale

Keep each piece with its labelled page until assembly. Stack parts from the lowest layer number (back) to the highest (front); the layer number is printed below each part name. The following connection guide matches the numbered joints in this overview.

Cut the outer contours, keeping the rounded overlap on both parts. Punch the matching numbered holes, then stack the back piece under the front piece and align the holes. Pass one paper fastener through both holes; leave it loose enough to turn. Fixed joins use adhesive overlap. Blue crosses mark rod attachments.

Make a paper prototype first. Turn every joint through its intended movement. Keep both rounded ends; check for gaps, catching and loose holes. Hold the puppet against a backlit screen to check dark overlaps and readable detail. Added overlap uses the piece color; hidden artwork is not reconstructed. These classroom joint dimensions still need practitioner and material testing. A planar preview does not test strength, fastener thickness or out-of-plane movement.
